🚀 Recommended Stack for Your 7-Day MVP

Since you're aiming to launch quickly, I would keep the stack as small as possible:

Weather: Open-Meteo (completely free, no API key) or WeatherAPI (very generous free tier)
Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim
Maps: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap
Air Quality: Open-Meteo
Sunrise/Sunset: Open-Meteo (included)
AI: OpenAI or Groq
Authentication: Clerk or Auth.js
Database: MongoDB Atlas (fits your MERN experience)
Notifications: OneSignal
Email: Resend
Payments: Stripe (add when you're ready to monetize)

This combination minimizes costs, reduces integration time, and is sufficient to validate whether users find your AI-powered weather recommendations valuable before investing in more advanced APIs.
